## Overview
After our failed confrontation of [[The Scrapper]] we had to agree to get a [[Runic tattoo]].

This tattoo binds us to a deal
1. [[The Scrapper]] will clear out the [[Court of Arms]] within 24 hours for 3 weeks. 
	1. This will allow us to tell [[The Group]] in [[The Grove]] that we succeeded in clearing them out
2. We will have to kill 1 person that [[The Scrapper]] can choose at any time. We will have 72 hours to complete the killing after being notified
3. If we fail to kill the target we die 
4. If [[The Scrapper]] does not hold up his end the pact is void.
