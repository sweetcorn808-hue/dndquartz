# Main Quest

## Overview
**Our party** has signed up for a secret mission organized by the [[The Hands of the Clock (HOC)]]. They do not yet know what it entails, only that it is of great importance to [[Ammerwael]] and it could lead to influence, money, access, and maybe even redemption. But there are rumors the journey will lead them straight into the heart of the heretical [[Panacea]]. (Spoiler alert: it does.)
