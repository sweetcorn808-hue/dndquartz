## Recap 
- Party reunited in The Grove and explored the city seeking information
- A general store merchant revealed the [[Medallion of the Fist]] (brown copper medallion with a gauntlet and led them through a secret door)
- Entered an underground black market run by the [[Court of Arms]]
- Hobbs noticed the magitek on display was crude DIY knockoffs, not real, obviously smuggled out of [[Ammerwael]] and being sold on the mainland
- At a book stall, Hobbs found the [[Chronicle of Panacea]] — a page inside referenced **Valeria**, written in Infernal
- A halfling pickpocket crew attempted to scam the party — Lennox and Bud caught two of them
- Shook down the halflings for a **map of the Court**, revealing the full bazaar layout
- The map shows a central alcove belonging to the Court's leader — [[The Scrapper]]

**Loose Ends**

- Who or what is Valeria?
- The Scrapper — next point of contact?
- The Medallion of the Fist and its ties to the Court of Arms and the smuggling of Magitek
