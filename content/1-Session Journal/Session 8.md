### Session 7 Recap

- **Magic doesn't work in the Court of Arms** — Hobbs' invisibility failed immediately upon entry; vendors use special bracelets with limited charges to access magic, the party managed to obtain one for Hobbs
- **Kidnapped a shopkeeper** — Rock knocked out a vendor to use as a referral to get into the Scrapper's office, Lennox talked away suspicious cops on the way back to the hotel
- **Hobbs scouted invisible** and found the tunnel to the Scrapper's office — 20ft, 90 degree turn, 75ft corridor, door guarded by **Starsky and Brian**
- **The meeting with the Scrapper went sideways** — he saw through the party's cover story immediately, pulled a pistol, and when negotiations broke down combat ensued
- **The party was forced into a Runic Pact** [[Runic tattoo]] — the Scrapper will clear out the Court of Arms within 3 weeks if the party kills **someone of his choosing within 72 hours**, failure on either end means death
