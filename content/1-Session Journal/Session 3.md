## Session Overview 
We start in the catamaran craft hurtling toward the ground after departing [[Ammerwael]].

Rock sees some shiny buttons and pushes them.
	The nose evens out after he pushes the button and the craft becomes level.
Hobbs is trying to find flight control instruction's to relay to Bud.
Radio comes to life with an authoritative voice saying the craft must return to [[Ammerwael]]. A remote override was activated and it begins to return. Rock kills the power and we set up some parachutes to make a sail and begin falling towards the surface as the descent. 

We drop below the clouds and begin to see the surface for the first time, for three of us.
We see more land than we have ever seen. oceans in the distance, cities and villages. Mountains in the distance.
We see 1 lone tower that looks like a clock tower in the distance. Miles away.

We end up landing in a boggy area. Not very close to any civilization. 
The craft crashed in the immediate area but was destroyed.

[[Rock]] and [[Bud]] hear rustling near us while we try to gather our senses and make for the closest tree line about 1/2 mile away. 

As [[Bud]] and [[Hobbs]] are bickering Bud fall to the ground and starts getting dragged away.
Hobbs flanks to see what happened but can only hear her being dragged away faster than you would expect

Hobbs also notices a layer of mist low along the ground that is very dense, I did not notice before.
Lennox falls unconscious from the mist, (nobody knows why or even sees it for now), she is on Rocks shoulders.
