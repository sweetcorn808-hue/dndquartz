### Session 5 Recap

- Party sought out [[Saulvin Sparrow]] at the greenhouse to help translate the [[Chronicle of Panacea]]
- Sal introduced the party to [[Doc]], a potion maker, who partially translated the book — key words: _City, Valeria, From the before time, Averial_
- **Major Reveal: The Grove IS Valeria** — Doc confirmed it while showing an illustration of the area
- Learned the [[Maze Engine]] is buried underground, its records lost to time 
- The fountain water is tied to Valeria's magical core
- **Hobbs saw real magic for the first time and had a panic attack** — notably, nobody followed or checked on him
- At the library, learned **Magitek is illegal in The Grove** and that the Maze Engine may grant wishes or create an anti-magic field
- Walking through City Hall, the party was approached by a guard and brought before **The Group** (the ruling council of 4)
- The Group knows the party's cover story is false, but want to make a deal:
    - **Task:** Clear out the Court of Arms
    - **Reward:** Information on the **Golden Scope**, supplies, and gold
- Learned the [[The Golden Scope]] is already in the hands of the **Mayor of [[Theoria]] 
- Intel on Court of Arms: smugglers with city-wide reach, Scrapper builds the tech: take out the head and the network likely collapses
