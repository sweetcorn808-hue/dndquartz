Bud tries to somewhat betray us and betrays Hobbs identity to the hobgoblins. Say they can hold [[Hobbs]] ransom if they get us down to the surface. Things get very intense with the conversation. people in the gym begin to start bringing around. Mind read they could use some help finding the [[Red Slad]].

We hope on the shuttle and barely get away from the cops. We are now hurtling towards the land in a stolen shuttle.
