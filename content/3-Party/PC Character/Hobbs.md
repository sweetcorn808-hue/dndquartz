## Story
Hobbs is the youngest of the [[Ariluth Family]] children. He was born late in his parents lives and was most likely not a planned birth. As the family has aged and drifted further from the Averial faith and life the characteristic wings have disappeared from most of the younger children, Hobbs included, although Hobbs is notable the first noble birth without wings. 

This has driven a lot of the turmoil in Hobbs towards his family and he has also received more condescension as not being a proper Averial. He was born into the highest tier of society but without wings has been unable to access all of it. He has had to try to prove him self worthy of his family from an early age. This has brough about a superiority/inferiority complex. He is now a symbol of what the city and society has chosen to become without the reliance on the old Averial Gods. 

He has a had a string of poorly received inventions. He is genuinely a studious and talented magitek user and inventor and has studied history of [[Ammerwael]] and the Averial more than most. He simply applies none of this knowledge in moments it would help and all of it in moments where it makes things worse. 

Although he his a full member of the Ariluth family he has always sensed he is in someway a second class citizen in his own house. Not disliked by any means but tolerated and humored. 

## Motivation and Philosophy
He is expected to be a full and competent member of the family, regardless of how is treated behind the doors of the estate. 

Will do anything to prove and secure his place in the family and society.

**Mental Traits**: Ambitious, intelligent, Analytical, Creative, Reckless, desperate.

## Personal Life
There is no disaster in his personal history, only dramatic backstory. He is always in the middle of a redemption arc. He has been in the middle of one since approximately age twelve

## Professional Life
Depending on who you ask he is either the most talented magitek illusionist or a wealthy eccentric who puts on shows no one asked for. 

His illusions are spectacular, frequently impractical, and made through a combination of genuine technical study and instincts he cannot explain.
