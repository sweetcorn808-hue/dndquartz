## Background
starting location. Flying city-state pictured above. As far as vibe goes, think less steampunk and more Howl's Moving Castle. 

Centuries before, Ammerwael was founded and built by sacred Avariel (winged elves.) Before Ammerwael, these elves helped build **Panacea** under the guidance of their air goddess, [[Aerdrie Faenya]]. The Avariel shepherded races of all kinds to Panacea, in hopes of creating a land of peace and harmony. As the population grew, the citizens gave into greed, fear, and violence. The Avariel did their best to prevent bloodshed, but to no avail. They turned to Aerdrie for help, who offered little but patience.

Eventually the pain became too much and they created Ammerwael. Ammerwael was meant to be everything Panacea was not with two notable changes: no more gods; no more magic. They discovered a new deity of a sort - science. Using technology, they built their floating eden and the last of their acolytes began this new community.  

Everything evolved in the following centuries. Their technology - fused with a previously undiscovered magic - became the lifeforce of Ammerwael. It gave them electricity, indoor lights, mass production, and more.   

Ammerwael cut itself off from Panacea. Most of the citizens have never been on the Walking World. The only people that leave are merchants and missionaries, out to collect goods and more acolytes.
